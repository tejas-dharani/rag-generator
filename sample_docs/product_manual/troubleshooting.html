Troubleshooting the AquaPure X2

Indicator lights

  • Solid blue: normal operation.
  • Flashing blue: the unit is running its 10-minute self-clean cycle. Do not dispense water until it finishes.
  • Solid amber: the carbon filter has less than 15% of its life left. Order a replacement (part CF-200).
  • Flashing amber: the carbon filter is expired and must be replaced now.
  • Solid red: UV lamp fault. Stop using the unit and contact support.
  • Flashing red: leak detected. The inlet valve closes automatically.

Error codes

CodeMeaningWhat to do
E1Low water pressure (below 1.0 bar)Check that the inlet tap is fully open and the inlet hose is not kinked.
E2Water temperature above 38 °CConnect the unit to the cold water supply only.
E3Filter not seatedRemove the filter and re-insert it until it clicks.
E4UV lamp failureContact support; the UV lamp is not user-replaceable.
E7Leak sensor triggeredDry the base tray, check hose connections, then hold the power button for 5 seconds to reset.

Slow flow

If flow drops below 1 litre per minute, the membrane may be clogged. Run a manual flush by holding the Flush button for 3 seconds. If flow does not recover, replace the RO membrane (part RM-75).

Strange taste

A slight plastic taste is normal for the first 48 hours after installing a new filter. Flush 10 litres of water through the unit to remove it. A persistent metallic or musty taste usually means the carbon filter is overdue for replacement.

Contacting support

Email support@aquapure.example or call 0800 555 0199, Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00. Have your serial number ready; it is printed on the label under the water tank.
