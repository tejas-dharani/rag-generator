# Brightleaf Analytics — Leave Policy

*Fictional company. Sample document for the RAG Generator demo. Policy version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026.*

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours; for example, an employee working 3 days a week receives 14.4 days.

Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month for full-time staff. New joiners may take leave once they have completed their 60-day probation period, although managers can approve earlier leave in exceptional cases.

Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Unused leave cannot be exchanged for cash, except when an employee leaves the company, in which case accrued and untaken leave is paid out in the final salary.

## Requesting Leave

All leave must be requested through the PeopleHub portal. Requests of up to 3 days require at least **1 week's notice**. Requests of more than 3 consecutive days require at least **4 weeks' notice**. Managers should respond to requests within 3 working days.

During the December freeze (15–31 December), no more than 30% of any team may be on leave at the same time. Requests during this period are approved on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave does not carry over. A doctor's certificate is required for absences longer than **3 consecutive days**. Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive **20 weeks of fully paid parental leave**. Secondary caregivers receive **6 weeks of fully paid leave**, which may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption. Employees become eligible after 6 months of continuous employment.

## Other Leave

- **Bereavement leave:** 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member, 2 days for extended family.
- **Volunteering leave:** 2 paid days per year to volunteer with a registered charity.
- **Study leave:** up to 5 paid days per year for exams related to an approved qualification.
- **Jury duty:** paid at full salary for the duration of service.
